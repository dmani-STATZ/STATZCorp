Supplier replies in the Quotes mailbox now have a **Scan quote** button. It reads the email and its PDF or image attachments and fills the Log quote tray for you: part number offered, CAGE, unit cost, days ARO, minimum order and payment terms, plus a short "Scan:" line in Notes with the quote's validity, FOB point and condition. Anything that looks off (math that doesn't add up, a different quantity or solicitation, FOB Origin) is highlighted so you can check it. Press it again for a deeper scan if something was missed. The scan never saves anything: review the tray and press Save as usual.
