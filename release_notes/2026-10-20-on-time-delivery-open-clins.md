The On-Time Delivery Report now includes CLINs that haven't fully shipped, so past-due open CLINs are no longer hidden.

**How open CLINs are counted.** A CLIN that is unshipped or only partly shipped counts as **late** once its due date has passed. If the due date hasn't arrived yet, it is **not yet due** and is left out of the Late / On-Time percentages. Open CLINs are checked against today's date, which is shown above the table. Rerunning an old month can change the numbers as open CLINs age.

**New Not Shipped column.** Each row shows how many of its CLINs are not fully shipped. Notes under the table tell you how many CLINs were not yet due.

**Excel export.** The Summary tab adds an **As Of** date and the Not Shipped column. The Detail tab adds **Status**, **Shipped Qty / Order Qty**, and an **Early Ship Flag** that reads **Check** when a CLIN shipped more than 180 days before its supplier due date. Late columns can now read **Not Yet Due**.
