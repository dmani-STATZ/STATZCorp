You can now find IDIQ (long-term) contracts from **Jump to Contract** (Ctrl+K) and the search box on the Contracts Dashboard.

**IDIQ Contracts panel.** When your search matches an IDIQ number, an **IDIQ Contracts** section appears above the contract results. Each row shows the IDIQ number, an **Open** or **Closed** badge, how many delivery orders it has, and its award date. Click a row to open the IDIQ. Dashes, spaces, and upper/lower case don't matter. Up to 25 IDIQs are listed, exact matches first. If there are more, the panel tells you how many.

**Delivery order badge.** Contracts that are delivery orders under an IDIQ now show an **IDIQ** badge with the parent number. Click it to go straight to the IDIQ.
