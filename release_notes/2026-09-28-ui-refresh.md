## What Changed
STATZ has a new coat of paint. Everything works the same way, it just looks
cleaner and is easier to read.

- **New header and sidebar.** The sidebar is grouped into **Operations**,
  **Bidding**, **Insights** and **Tools**, and highlights the page you're on.
  Press **Esc** to close it.
- **Easier-to-read text.** Body text in light mode is darker, and cards,
  borders and shadows are more consistent from page to page.
- **Better dark mode.** Dark mode uses cool slate tones instead of flat grey,
  and pages that used to show bright white panels in dark mode now match.
- **Cleaner buttons.** Buttons are flatter and look the same across the app.
- **Redesigned pages:** sign-in, home, About, Training, Inventory and the
  STATZ Quotes dashboard.

## What You Need To Do
Nothing. If something looks off on a page you use, let us know.
