## What Changed
Quotes has a new **Quotes** page in Phase 2, right after the Mailbox. It is where you see who still owes us a quote, review and edit every quote on file, and enter a quote that did not come by email (a phone call, a fax, a supplier website).

**Waiting on suppliers** lists every RFQ we sent that has no quote yet, soonest-due first, with how long we have been waiting. If a supplier replied but nobody entered their quote it says so and links to their message. **Enter quote** opens the same side tray as the Mailbox. **No response** and **Declined…** (with a reason) close a supplier out so they stop showing; **Back to waiting** undoes it.

**Logged quotes** shows every quote on file with how it arrived ("Phone · Sep 29 · Sam"), what it covers and where its bid stands. **Edit** opens it in the tray. **Remove** deletes a quote that no bid is using.

**Enter a quote** takes any solicitation and any supplier (the ones we asked are listed first). The tray adds **Received via** (phone, fax, email, website, other), **Date received** and **Spoke with / reference #**.

## What You'll Notice
- A supplier has one quote per line. Entering a revised quote, from a later email or by phone, updates the one on file instead of adding a second that competes with it. Opening the tray on a solicitation the supplier already quoted shows that quote.
- The quote picker in the tray now only appears when there is something to choose, for example when a supplier priced the lines separately. A new quote can only cover lines the supplier has not quoted yet.
- Entering a quote for a supplier we sent an RFQ to marks that RFQ answered, so they leave the waiting list.
- A quote already sent to DIBBS is shown read-only, with the file it went out in, on both pages. Removing a quote is refused while any bid uses it.
- The **Bid** column on Logged quotes tells you the next step: **Build bid** opens the bid builder, **Continue** finishes a draft, **Export** goes to the BQ Export when the bid is ready. There is no separate "send to DIBBS" switch: a solicitation shows on the Bid Board as soon as it has a quote, and **Save & mark ready** in the builder is what stages it.
- The Bid Board now says when open solicitations with quotes are hidden because every bid on them was already exported to DIBBS, and points at the Submitted tab. Its empty states spell out the steps.
- Under a message, the quotes table now lists the sender's quotes on those solicitations, and says where a quote came from when it was not this message.

## Action Required
None. Quotes saved before this change show as email quotes. If a supplier already has two quotes on one line from earlier testing, the Logged quotes tab flags it **Duplicate** so you can remove the old one.
