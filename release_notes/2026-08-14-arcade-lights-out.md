The **STATZ Daily Arcade** is now available as a hidden feature within STATZWeb.

## What's New

- **Shared Daily Puzzles:** Every user receives the exact same puzzle generated deterministically for each day.
- **Lights Out:** The first playable game features a 5x5 grid with weekday difficulty scaling (Monday easiest through Saturday hardest).
- **Server-Authoritative Grading:** Move validation and par scoring are calculated on the server with full payload redaction.
- **Daily Leaderboard & Handicap Tracking:** Compete on daily completion times and track your average clicks over par (handicap displayed after 5 qualifying attempts).
- **Resume Support:** In-progress games persist server-side and active play time is accumulated with automatic idle capping.
- **Arcade Lobby:** Access the lobby featuring registered game cards and daily leaders.
