## What Changed
**STATZ Quotes** is our new quoting app. You'll find it in the sidebar under
**Bidding**. It covers the whole quoting cycle, from a new DIBBS solicitation to
the bid file we upload, and then shows whether we won.

1. **Solicitations**: every open DIBBS solicitation, matched to the suppliers
   who can quote it.
2. **RFQ Queue**: one email per supplier, sent from the quotes mailbox with the
   solicitation PDFs attached.
3. **Mailbox**: supplier replies are matched to their solicitation. Log each
   quote with packaging, freight and markup.
4. **Bid Board**: compare quotes side by side, build the DIBBS bid and export
   the BQ upload file.
5. **Our Bids**: see which submitted bids were won or lost, and by how much.

New DIBBS solicitations flow into STATZ Quotes automatically every day. Each
step has its own release note with more detail.

## What You Need To Do
Nothing to set up. Suppliers need a **Sales** contact (or an email on the
supplier record) to receive RFQs.
