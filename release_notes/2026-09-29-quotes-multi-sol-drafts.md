## What Changed
When one supplier email covers more than one solicitation, the **Log supplier quote** drawer now keeps what you type for each SOL with that SOL. Before, switching SOLs left the previous SOL's numbers in the form, and packaging or freight totals typed for one SOL were spread over the next SOL's quantity, which gave a wrong Final Gov Price.

## What You'll Notice
- Switching SOLs in the drawer gives you a clean form for that SOL, and switching back restores exactly what you entered.
- Your entries are saved as you go. Close the drawer, open another message, or reload the page and they are still there. A saved quote clears only its own SOL's entries. **Clear this SOL's entries** starts one over.
- The SOL list shows **• draft** and **✓ quote logged**, and the drawer opens on the first SOL that has no quote from this message yet.
- Packaging and freight say how many units a total is being spread over, and the price box shows the extended amount (price × quantity), so you can see the quantity being used.
- Changing between **Combined** and **Split CLINs**, or picking another line, now recalculates the per-unit or total figure you did not type, instead of leaving a stale number beside it.
- The drawer no longer dims the page behind it, so you can keep reading the message while you type.

## Action Required
None. Entries in progress are kept in your browser, so they are not visible to other people or on another computer.
