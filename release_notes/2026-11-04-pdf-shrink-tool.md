PDF tools now has a Shrink PDF page.

Use it on files that were saved with Microsoft Print to PDF. A 43 MB copy can come back at about 1.5 MB.

The pages stay readable and printable, but the text can't be selected or searched afterward, and gray or color areas become black or white.

Starting a new RFQ? Download the original from DIBBS and use Fill PDF instead.
