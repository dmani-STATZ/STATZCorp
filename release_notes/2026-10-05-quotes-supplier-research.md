New: Supplier Research page (Quotes → Supplier Research). Enter a CAGE code to see SAM.gov data, approved-source capabilities, and DLA award history, and download it all as a three-tab Excel workbook. The page opens right away and each section loads on its own, so a slow SAM.gov lookup no longer holds up the rest. If a SAM.gov lookup fails you can retry it with one click. Duplicate award and approved-source rows are now collapsed, and placeholder awards are left out.
