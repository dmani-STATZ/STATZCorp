Superusers have a new **User Access** page. Open it from **Workspace Settings**, next to Admin Console.

- Create users ahead of their first Microsoft sign-in.
- Grant or remove app access.
- Assign CMMC training account types and see Arctic Wolf tracking status.
- Disable a user to cut off all access right away. Their grants and training history are kept, and **Reactivate** brings them back.

Disabled accounts can no longer sign in with Microsoft, and any session they already have open ends on their next request.

The Training app grant controls entry when Training is registered and app permission checks are enabled. CMMC account types determine required courses. Arctic Wolf tracking follows Staff status and course eligibility follows the user's join date.
