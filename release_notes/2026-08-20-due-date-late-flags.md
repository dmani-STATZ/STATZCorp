Due-date highlighting now updates from actual shipment timing. Revising a due
date immediately clears the red late styling when the completed shipment is no
longer late.

Closed and canceled contracts no longer inflate the dashboard's past-due
count, and the count now reflects every open contract whose due date has
passed.
