Paid Date and Customer Pay Date can be entered when adding or editing a shipment. Each has a Today button, and both show as columns in the shipments window. When you enter a Paid or Customer Pay amount, its payment history row uses that date. On a CLIN with shipments, the CLIN Paid Date and Customer Received dates now come from the latest shipment dates and are changed on the shipments.
