## What Changed
**STATZ Quotes** now has its first working screens.

- **Solicitations** — every open DIBBS solicitation in Unmatched, Matched and
  RFQ Sent tabs. Filter instantly by set-aside, due date and estimated value
  (quantity × the last price DLA paid). Rows show which suppliers are linked and
  why (NSN, FSC or Manual), and who else is reviewing a solicitation right now.
- **Solicitation workspace** — lines, approved sources, recent DLA purchases and
  linked suppliers. Link an approved source in one click, or search the supplier
  directory and optionally save the NSN or FSC to that supplier so future
  solicitations match automatically.
- **Work the list** — pick a filtered list and step through it one
  solicitation at a time with **Next**, **No bid & next** or **Queue RFQ & next**
  (keyboard: N / P). If a teammate is already on the next solicitation, it is
  skipped automatically and you are told who has it, so two people can work the
  same list without colliding.
- **RFQ Queue** — one email per supplier covering every solicitation queued to
  them, sent from the quotes mailbox with the solicitation PDFs attached.

Solicitations left unmatched for 7 days, or past their due date, archive
automatically.

## What You Need To Do
Nothing to set up. Suppliers need a **Sales** contact (or an email on the
supplier record) to receive RFQs.
