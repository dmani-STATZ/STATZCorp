An Excel icon in the Contracts section of any supplier's detail page downloads a spreadsheet of every CLIN assigned to that supplier—open, closed, and cancelled—with status, PO and IDIQ numbers, contract number, buyer, NSN, dates, and quantities. The workbook opens with filters on the header row so you can slice open orders and other views in Excel without extra setup.
