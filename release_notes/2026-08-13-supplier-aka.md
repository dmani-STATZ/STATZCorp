Supplier records now support **Also Known As** names (trade names, DBAs, and legacy names) so you can find a supplier even when the name you remember is not the official one.

- **Add and remove aliases** on the supplier detail page. A supplier can have none, one, or many.
- **Dashboard search, list search, and autocomplete** all match against these aliases as well as the official name.
- Aliases are entered by hand. They are not filled in by DIBBS or other imports.
