The Inventory page has a new look. Cards at the top show Total Value, Items, Total Units and how many items are listed publicly. Search by NSN, description, part number, manufacturer or location (results update as you type), filter by public or private, and choose 25, 50 or 100 rows per page. Click any column header to sort; sorting and filters stay in place as you page through results. Edit and delete are icon buttons on each row, and delete asks for confirmation first. Editing an item with a blank quantity or price no longer causes an error. The Add Item and Edit Item screen has been redesigned to match, and Add Item includes a **Save & add another** button.
