## What Changed
**Our Bids** shows how every bid we submitted through STATZ Quotes turned out.

- Each hour the system matches submitted bids to the awards DIBBS publishes and
  marks them **Won** or **Lost** (or **Pending** until the award posts). Use
  **Check awards now** to run it on demand.
- The cards at the top show bids won and lost, win rate, and the
  **"Within 5%"** misses: bids we lost by 5% or less. Switch between Week,
  Month, Quarter and All Time instantly.
- **Details** on any bid shows who won and at what price, exactly how we priced
  our bid at the time (supplier, packaging, freight, markup), and pointers such
  as whether a lower markup would have won.

DIBBS publishes award totals rather than unit prices, so the award price shown
is the total divided by the quantity on our line.
