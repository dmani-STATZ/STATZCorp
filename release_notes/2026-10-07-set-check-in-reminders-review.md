When you mark the PO as sent to the supplier, STATZ now asks whether you want to set reminders instead of creating them automatically. Open **Set Reminders** from that prompt or from the Contract Review page to see suggested dates for the acknowledgment follow-up, first supplier check-in, mid-contract check-in, and 30-day pre-due reminders. You can change each date, choose whether it applies to the whole contract or a specific CLIN, skip rows you do not need, or add your own reminder dates. Saved reminders still appear as notes with reminder pills on the contract, the same as before.

Bell icons beside the contract title and each CLIN on Contract Management show reminder status at a glance: outline white/green means no pending reminders, solid green means reminders are set, and red means something is overdue (due today still shows green). The contract bell rolls up reminders on the contract and all of its CLINs.

Click any bell to open the reminders window for that contract or CLIN. You can complete, delete, or extend a reminder (+3 days, +1 week, or a date you pick), or add a new reminder with a label and date. Changes stay in sync with the footer reminder pill and your notes list.
