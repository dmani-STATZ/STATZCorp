## What Changed
When a new modification posts on DIBBS for one of our contracts, STATZ now
emails the contract's reviewer automatically. If the contract has no reviewer,
the Contract Administrators get the email instead.

The email lists the mod date, price, NSN and a link to the DIBBS award record,
plus a link straight to the contract in STATZ. Acknowledge the mod on the
contract page as usual.
