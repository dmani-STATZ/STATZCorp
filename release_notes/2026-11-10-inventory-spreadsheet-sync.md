The Inventory page has a new **Sync spreadsheet** button. Upload your inventory workbook as it is, and STATZWeb shows a preview of exactly what will be added, updated and removed before anything changes. Press **Apply changes**, then **Download updated spreadsheet** to get a fresh copy that looks like yours (your other tabs, like PurchaseOrders, come along untouched). Work from that file and repeat whenever you like. Items someone adds on the Inventory page between syncs are kept, not deleted, and show up in your next download.

Items now have a **Qty Remaining** field for bulk lots that get drawn down over time. Leave it blank when Quantity is already the count on hand.

The dashboard's **On-Hand Value** card and the spreadsheet's new **ON-HAND VALUE** row now reflect what is actually on the shelf, using Qty Remaining where it's filled in. The spreadsheet's **TOTAL** row is now a true sum of every row.
