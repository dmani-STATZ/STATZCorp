## What Changed
**STATZ Quotes** now has a **Mailbox** tab for supplier replies to the quotes inbox.

- **Check for new mail** pulls in new replies. Replies that mention a
  solicitation number (our RFQs always do) are matched to that solicitation and
  supplier automatically. Anything we could not match is flagged **No SOL** so
  you can link it by hand.
- Click a message to read it without leaving the list, or use the up/down
  arrow keys to move through the inbox. Filters show only unmatched mail,
  mail still needing a quote, or unread mail.
- **Log supplier quote** opens a side panel: enter the supplier's price, lead
  time and part number, add packaging and freight (per unit or as a total),
  and pick a 2 / 4 / 6 % markup or type your own price. The government unit
  price calculates as you type.
- Saving records the quote against every line of the solicitation (or one line
  with **Split CLINs**), marks the supplier's RFQ as answered, and picks the
  lowest-cost quote for the bid automatically unless you have already chosen one.

## What You Need To Do
Nothing. For safety, message content is shown with images and scripts from
the sender blocked, and attachments download rather than open in the browser
(PDFs excepted).
