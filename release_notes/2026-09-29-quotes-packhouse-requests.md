## What Changed
When you log a supplier's quote and the packaging is done by a third-party packhouse, you can now ask the packhouses for a price without leaving the drawer, then use their answer.

**Ask:** in the **Packaging** section, choose *Third-party packhouse*. Under **Packhouse quotes for this SOL**, click **Email packhouses for a quote**, pick one or more packhouses (packhouses used on this NSN before are one click away), add a note if you want, and **Preview** before you **Send**. Each packhouse gets its own email from the shared quotes mailbox with the item, quantity, weight and dimensions, the solicitation's packaging requirements, and the solicitation PDF.

**Record:** when a packhouse replies, it shows up in the Mailbox like any other message and its request is marked *Replied*. Open it and type in the price they quoted (a total or a per-unit price, the other is worked out), plus days and notes.

**Use:** back in the supplier's quote drawer, a packhouse with a recorded price has a **Use** button. It selects the packhouse and fills in the per-unit packaging price.

**Weight & dimensions** now has its own section in the drawer, above Packaging and Freight. Packaging and Freight both use it, so you enter it once.

## What You'll Notice
- The request list for the SOL is visible in the drawer, with who was asked, when, and what they quoted.
- A packhouse you are still waiting on will not be emailed again for the same request.
- A packhouse's reply no longer counts as a supplier quote waiting to be logged (it drops out of *Needs quote*).
- If weight and dimensions are blank, the preview warns you and the email asks the packhouse to tell you what they need.
- Sending never touches the solicitation's status.

## Action Required
None. Packhouses need an email address on file (a Sales contact, or the supplier's RFQ, business or primary email).
